Privacy Policy — AegisSOC Edge Interceptor

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 • Applies to AegisSOC Edge Interceptor (v2.6.1+) & Executive Console

1. Data Accessed During Attachment Inspection

AegisSOC Edge Interceptor only inspects attachments when you click to preview or download a file on web.whatsapp.com or mail.google.com, or when you explicitly select a file in the manual triage drop zone:

  • PDF Documents (.pdf): Structural inspection for active exploit triggers (/JavaScript, /Launch, /OpenAction, /EmbeddedFile) runs locally inside your browser. Only if a disguised or embedded Windows PE executable is detected inside the PDF is that binary slice sent for neural analysis.
  • PE Executables & Raster Images (.exe, .dll, .png, .jpg): Up to the first 256 KB header slice (or full file up to 4 MB) is transmitted securely over HTTPS to our inference endpoint (teem5002-aegissoc-backend.hf.space) solely to compute malware classification and Shannon entropy scores.

2. Zero Data Retention & No Tracking

All inspected file buffers are processed strictly in volatile memory during real-time triage and discarded immediately after returning the JSON verdict. AegisSOC does not log, store to disk, sell, profile, or share your files, emails, chat messages, contacts, credentials, or browsing history with any third party.

3. Local Session Caching

To prevent duplicate scans between opening a preview and downloading the same attachment, SHA-256 hashes and scan verdicts are cached temporarily inside your local browser session memory. Widget drag coordinates are stored locally in your browser's localStorage.

4. Permissions Transparency

  • downloads: Holds unverified Gmail attachment downloads until security inspection completes.
  • storage: Stores local UI preferences and extension state.
  • tabs: Sends real-time scan notifications to the active Gmail tab HUD.

5. Contact & Source Verification

AegisSOC is open-source. For privacy questions or security reports, visit github.com/doog-listener/aegissoc.
